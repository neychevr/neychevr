Hi, I'm Radoslav. I teach machine learning and lead an LLM research group at the Yandex AI Lab.

## Courses

* [**lectures.girafe.ai**](https://lectures.girafe.ai): my open lectures in one place: ML, NLP, computer vision, and RL, with video, slides, and homework.
* [**Yandex ML Trainings**](https://yandex.ru/yaintern/training/ml-training): a free public course I design and teach every year; 16k+ registrations in the first season.
* [**Machine Learning handbook**](https://ml-handbook.ru): the Yandex textbook on ML; I am a co-author.
* [**MSAI**](https://eng.mipt.ru/programs/ai/): the international online master's program in AI at MIPT, which I founded in 2020. Taught in English; five cohorts have graduated.
